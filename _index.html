The Art of Parallax

Parallax scrolling creates a sense of depth by moving background images at different speeds than foreground images. This technique has been used in video games since the 1980s and has become increasingly popular in web design.

The effect creates an illusion of depth in a 2D scene, making the experience more immersive and engaging for users. As you scroll down this page, notice how different elements move at different rates, creating a dynamic and visually interesting experience.
